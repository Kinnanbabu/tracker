iPhone Buying Guide

  • Base vs Pro: Get the Pro if you want 120Hz ProMotion screen and 5x optical zoom.
  • Storage: Don't buy 128GB if you take 4K video. 256GB is the sweet spot.
  • Savings Tip: Wait for festive sales (Diwali / Amazon Great Indian Festival) for ₹10,000 - ₹15,000 bank card discounts!

MacBook Pro Buying Guide

  • RAM is King: Always choose at least 16GB or 18GB/24GB Unified Memory. RAM cannot be upgraded later!
  • M3/M4 Pro Chip: Superb for heavy development, Docker, and video editing with zero fan noise.
  • Student Discount: Apple offers up to ₹10,000 off + free AirPods during the Back-to-School season!

How to Host on Vercel (Step-by-Step for Newbies)
  1. Push to GitHub: Create a new repository on github.com (e.g. named my-goal-planner) and upload these project files.
  2. Open Vercel: Go to vercel.com and sign in with your GitHub account (100% free).
  3. Import Repository: Click "Add New... > Project", select your GitHub repo, and click "Deploy".
  4. Done in 30 Seconds! Vercel gives you your own public URL (e.g., https://my-goal-planner.vercel.app) that you can open on your phone and laptop!
  5. Optional Cloud Storage: Inside your Vercel Dashboard, go to Storage > click Vercel KV or Postgres > click "Create". It will automatically link and enable serverless cloud sync!
